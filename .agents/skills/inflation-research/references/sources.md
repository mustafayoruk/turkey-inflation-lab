## Updating evidence
Before adopting an unfamiliar/new method, check its latest available primary version, publication status, sample/geography, assumptions and correction/retraction status when indicated. Prefer accessible full methods for implementation. Log what was actually read and what remains unverified.
The initial tiny model set, compute limits, Python tool choices and staged integration plan are local engineering conventions. Do not attribute those exact choices to scientific consensus.
